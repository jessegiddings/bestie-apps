# Party game: her host lines

Drafts for her to rewrite in her own words. Say them however feels natural; the app shows whatever text is in `apps/party-game/src/content/host.ts`, so send back edits and we'll match the captions to what she actually says.

- Player names are never spoken, so lines say "you" while the screen shows the name.
- Record each line as its own file named after its id (for example `open-1.m4a`). Leave a half second of silence at each end.
- Her real voice only, no AI voice. No New Girl names, lines or games.
- 35 lines in all, about 15 to 20 minutes in the booth.
