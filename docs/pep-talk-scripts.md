# Her scripts: Pep talks ("Morning Bestie")

Drafts in a best-friend voice for her to rewrite. Change anything and say it the way she would. Timings assume about 150 words a minute.

In total: 30 morning notes, 30 moment talks and 8 charm notes, about 26 minutes of finished audio.

Recording tips: a quiet room with soft furnishings (a closet full of clothes is perfect), phone about 20cm away, Voice Memos is fine. Do two takes of each and keep the warmer one. Export as .m4a and name each file by its id, for example `morning-7.m4a`.

## Morning notes (one a day, in order)

**Morning 1: Hi, it’s me** (`morning-1`, ~35s) · free

Hey bestie! Okay, so this is how it works. Every morning, I'm going to leave you a little voice note, like a friend who's already had her coffee and is way too excited about your day. No homework, no pressure. You just press play while you brush your teeth or wait for the coffee. Today, I only want one thing from you: notice one good thing before lunch. That's it. A nice cloud, a decent parking spot, a dog. Tell me tomorrow. I'm so glad you're here.

**Morning 2: You came back** (`morning-2`, ~32s) · free

You came back! Honestly, that's my favorite part. Day two is where most people drift off, and here you are. So, did you find your good thing yesterday? I hope it was a dog. Today, I want you to say one nice thing to yourself in the mirror. Out loud. I know, I know. It feels silly. Do it anyway. 'You look nice today' counts. 'You're doing your best' counts. Silly is fine. Silly is how good habits start.

**Morning 3: The to-do list** (`morning-3`, ~28s) · free

Morning! Quick question: how long is your to-do list today? Okay, here's my trick. Pick the one thing that would make today feel like a win if it was the only thing you did. Just one. Do that first, before your phone eats your brain. Everything else is a bonus. You are not a machine, you're a person, and people get to have one big thing a day. Go get yours.

**Morning 4: Drink some water** (`morning-4`, ~26s) · free

Hi, bestie. This is your friendly reminder that you are basically a houseplant with feelings. Drink some water. Get some light on your face. Move a little bit. Half the time when I feel like everything's falling apart, I'm just thirsty and I haven't been outside. So before you decide your life is a mess, try the houseplant check first. Water, sunlight, stretch. Then we'll talk.

**Morning 5: Comparison** (`morning-5`, ~30s) · free

Okay, real talk this morning. If you're going to scroll today, and you are, because we all do, remember that everyone is posting their highlight reel. Nobody posts the bit where they cried in the car. So if you start feeling like everyone else has it figured out, they don't. I promise you, they don't. You're comparing your behind-the-scenes to their best take. Be nice to your behind-the-scenes. It's where all the good stuff gets made.

**Morning 6: Ask for it** (`morning-6`, ~24s) · free

Morning, you. Today's challenge: ask for something. A favor, a raise, help with the dishes, a better table, an extra pickle. It doesn't matter what. Most people never ask, so they never get. And the worst thing that happens is someone says no, and then you're exactly where you started, except braver. I want to hear about it. Go ask.

**Morning 7: One whole week** (`morning-7`, ~29s) · free

One whole week of mornings together! Can I just say, I'm proud of you. Seven days of showing up for yourself, even on the days you didn't feel like it. That's not nothing. That's actually the whole thing. You just earned a charm on your bracelet, and nobody can take it away. Now, go have a great day, and maybe treat yourself to a fancy coffee. You've earned it. I'll see you tomorrow.

**Morning 8: The hard thing** (`morning-8`, ~28s)

Hey, bestie. Is there something you've been putting off? A phone call, an email, a doctor's appointment? I know there is, because there always is. Here's the deal. Do it before ten a.m. today. Set a timer for ten minutes and just start. Usually the thing takes five minutes, and we've spent three weeks dreading it. Get it off your plate, and feel about ten pounds lighter by lunch.

**Morning 9: Your people** (`morning-9`, ~28s)

Morning! Today I want you to text someone you love and haven't talked to in a while. Not a big thing. Just 'thought of you today.' That's it. You'd be amazed what that does for someone. And you know what? It does just as much for you. We all think we're too busy for our people, but our people are kind of the whole point. Go send it. I'll wait.

**Morning 10: Messy is fine** (`morning-10`, ~27s)

Hi! Can I tell you a secret? I am not a perfectly put-together person. I lose my keys. I forget birthdays. I have, on more than one occasion, worn my shirt inside out all day. And I still think I'm pretty great. So if today gets messy, that's fine. Messy is normal. Messy is human. You don't need to be perfect to be wonderful. You already are.

**Morning 11: Say no** (`morning-11`, ~25s)

Good morning, bestie. Today's word is 'no.' Practice it. 'No, I can't make it.' 'No, that doesn't work for me.' 'No, thank you.' Full sentences, no essay needed. Every time you say yes to something you don't want, you're saying no to yourself. So today, protect your energy. You are allowed to be a little bit selfish with your time. It's yours.

**Morning 12: Ten minutes outside** (`morning-12`, ~23s)

Morning! Here's your mission: ten minutes outside today, no phone. Just walk. Around the block, to the corner store, around the parking lot, wherever. Look at the trees. Listen to whatever's going on. I know it sounds too simple to work, but it's basically magic for your brain. Ten minutes. You can do ten minutes. Report back.

**Morning 13: You’re allowed to rest** (`morning-13`, ~27s)

Hey, you. If you're tired today, I want you to hear this: rest is not lazy. Rest is not something you have to earn. You don't need to finish everything before you're allowed to sit down. Your body is not a phone you only charge at one percent. So if you need a slow day, have a slow day. The world will still be there after your nap.

**Morning 14: Two weeks** (`morning-14`, ~28s)

Two weeks! Two weeks of you and me, every morning. Okay, I'm getting a bit emotional. Seriously though, look at you. Two weeks ago this was a new thing, and now it's just something you do. That's how every good habit starts, one boring little morning at a time. You got another charm. I'm doing a little dance in my kitchen right now. You can't see it. It's very good.

**Morning 15: Compliments** (`morning-15`, ~26s)

Morning! Today, give out three compliments. Real ones. Tell the barista you love her earrings. Tell your coworker that was a great idea. Tell your mom her lasagna is the best. It costs nothing, it takes two seconds, and it makes someone's whole day. And here's the sneaky part: when you're busy noticing good things in other people, you start noticing them in yourself too.

**Morning 16: The inner critic** (`morning-16`, ~25s)

Hey bestie. That voice in your head that says you're not good enough? Let's give her a name. Something ridiculous. Like Brenda. So when she pipes up today, you can just go, 'Thanks, Brenda, not now.' She's not the boss of you. She's just loud. And you don't have to argue with her. Just let her talk, and carry on being brilliant anyway.

**Morning 17: Dress up a bit** (`morning-17`, ~22s)

Morning! Okay, small thing today. Wear something that makes you feel good. The good socks, the nice earrings, the jacket you're saving for a special occasion. Today is the special occasion. Why are we saving our favorite stuff for some day that might never come? Light the candle. Use the fancy plate. Today counts.

**Morning 18: Little wins** (`morning-18`, ~28s)

Hi, bestie. Tonight, before you go to sleep, I want you to write down three things you did today. Not big things. 'Made the bed.' 'Replied to that email.' 'Didn't yell at the printer.' We're so quick to list everything we didn't do, and we never give ourselves credit for what we did. So today, keep score of the wins. I bet it's a longer list than you think.

**Morning 19: Scared is fine** (`morning-19`, ~24s)

Morning, you. If there's something scary on your plate today, I want you to know: being scared doesn't mean you're not ready. It just means it matters. Everyone you admire was scared doing the thing that made them who they are. Every single one. So be scared, and do it anyway. That's what brave actually is. You've got this.

**Morning 20: Forgive yesterday** (`morning-20`, ~24s)

Hey. If yesterday didn't go great, let's leave it there. You don't have to drag it into today. Whatever you said, whatever you didn't do, whatever went sideways, it's done. Today is a whole fresh one. Brand new. Nobody's touched it yet. So take a breath, forgive yesterday's version of you, she was doing her best, and let's start over.

**Morning 21: Move your body** (`morning-21`, ~26s)

Morning, bestie! Put on one song you love. One. And dance to it. In the kitchen, in the shower, in the car at a red light. I don't care if you look ridiculous. Actually, I hope you do. Three minutes of moving like nobody's watching will do more for your mood than an hour of scrolling. Go on. Pick your song. Mine's on right now.

**Morning 22: Your own best friend** (`morning-22`, ~22s)

Morning! Here's a thought for today. Talk to yourself the way you'd talk to me. If I called you upset because I messed something up, you wouldn't say, 'Wow, you're useless.' You'd say, 'Oh, honey, that happens to everyone.' So say that to yourself today. You deserve a best friend on the inside too.

**Morning 23: Look up** (`morning-23`, ~24s)

Hi! Today, I want you to look up. Literally. Look up from your phone on the train, in line, at the lights. Look at the people, the buildings, the sky. We spend so much of our lives looking down. There's a whole world happening at eye level, and you're in it. Look up and notice one thing you've never noticed before.

**Morning 24: Money worries** (`morning-24`, ~26s)

Hey bestie. If money's on your mind this morning, you're not alone. Almost everyone worries about it, and almost nobody talks about it. So here's one small step: today, look at one number. Just one. Your balance, a bill, a subscription you forgot about. Looking is the hardest part. Once you've looked, it's a problem you can solve, not a cloud hanging over you.

**Morning 25: Good enough** (`morning-25`, ~24s)

Morning! Today's motto is 'good enough is good enough.' The email doesn't need a fourth draft. The dinner doesn't need to be from a cookbook. The workout doesn't need to be an hour. Done is better than perfect, and perfect is mostly a way to never finish anything. So do it good enough, and go enjoy the rest of your day.

**Morning 26: Laugh today** (`morning-26`, ~26s)

Hey you! Your only job today is to laugh properly at least once. Like, a real one, a snort if possible. Call the funniest person you know. Watch the video that always gets you. Remember that thing that happened at that wedding. Laughter is the fastest way back to yourself. And if all else fails, picture me trying to parallel park. That should do it.

**Morning 27: Be proud** (`morning-27`, ~26s)

Morning, bestie. Think about where you were a year ago. What were you worried about? What did you not know how to do yet? Now look at you. You've survived every hard day you've ever had. That's a perfect record. So today, I want you to be a little bit proud of yourself. Not for anything specific. Just for being you, still here, still trying.

**Morning 28: Sleep tonight** (`morning-28`, ~24s)

Hi! This morning, I'm actually thinking about tonight. Try going to bed thirty minutes earlier. Phone across the room, a book or a podcast instead, lights low. I know, revenge bedtime procrastination is real, and the quiet hours feel like the only ones that are yours. But you deserve to wake up tomorrow feeling like a human. Future you is begging.

**Morning 29: Dream a little** (`morning-29`, ~26s)

Morning! Okay, let's dream for a second. If nothing could go wrong, what would you do? Start the business, take the trip, learn the thing, move to the place. Say it out loud. Now, what's the tiniest first step? Not the whole plan. Just one step. Google it, email someone, save five dollars. Big dreams are just a lot of tiny steps in a row.

**Morning 30: A month together** (`morning-30`, ~30s)

One month! A whole month of mornings with me. I'm honestly so happy. Do you know how rare it is for someone to keep a promise to themselves for thirty days? You did that. Whatever else happened this month, the good days and the lousy ones, you kept showing up. That's a charm for your bracelet, and it's a big one. Thank you for letting me be part of your mornings. Let's keep going.

## "I need you" moments (three talks each, the first one free)

### Big day today

**Take 1** (`big-day-1`, ~29s)

Okay, it's the big day! First, breathe. In for four, hold for four, out for four. Good. Now listen to me. You are not walking in there to beg for anything. You're walking in there to show them what they get if they're lucky. You've prepared, you know your stuff, and even the bits you don't know, you'll figure out. Shoulders back. Smile at the first person you see. Go get it.

**Take 2** (`big-day-2`, ~22s)

Hey, superstar. Nervous? Good. That means you care, and caring is your secret weapon. Here's a trick: your body can't tell the difference between nerves and excitement. Same racing heart, same butterflies. So tell yourself, 'I'm excited.' Say it out loud. Now go in there and be excited. I'll be cheering from right here.

**Take 3** (`big-day-3`, ~22s)

Before you go in, I want you to remember three things you're really good at. Go on, name them. See? Those don't disappear because you're nervous. They come in the room with you. Whatever happens today, you showed up, and that's the part most people never manage. Call me after. I want every detail.

### Rough day

**Take 1** (`bad-day-1`, ~26s)

Oh, bestie. Come here. Rough days are rough, and I'm not going to tell you to look on the bright side. Sometimes it's just a bad day, and that's allowed. So here's the plan: get through the next hour. Just the next hour. Something warm to drink, something comfy to wear, and permission to do the bare minimum tonight. Tomorrow's a new one. I promise.

**Take 2** (`bad-day-2`, ~24s)

Hey. I know today's been a lot. Can I ask you to do one thing? Put your hand on your chest and take three slow breaths. Feel that? That's you, still here, still going, even on a terrible day. That's pretty amazing actually. You don't have to fix everything tonight. You just have to be kind to yourself until bedtime.

**Take 3** (`bad-day-3`, ~22s)

Okay, rough day protocol. One: eat something. Real food, not just crackers over the sink. Two: text one person and tell them it's been a bad day. You don't need to explain. Three: early night, no doom-scrolling. Bad days end. This one will too. And I'll be here in the morning with a fresh one.

### I feel anxious

**Take 1** (`anxious-1`, ~24s)

Hey, I'm here. Let's slow down together. Find five things you can see. Say them out loud. Now four things you can touch. Three things you can hear. Two things you can smell. One thing you can taste. Okay. You're here, in this room, and you're safe right now. Anxiety tells big stories. You don't have to believe all of them.

**Take 2** (`anxious-2`, ~24s)

Hi, bestie. Breathe with me. In through your nose, slowly, two, three, four. Hold it, two, three, four. And out through your mouth, really slowly, all the way out. Again. Your body thinks there's a lion in the room, and we're just showing it there isn't. One more. There you go. Feeling a little bit better? Stay with me.

**Take 3** (`anxious-3`, ~35s)

Okay, can I tell you what I do when my brain won't stop? I write it all down. Every single worry, as messy as it comes out. Then I look at the list and circle the ones I can actually do something about today. Usually it's one or two. The rest can wait outside. Try it. Paper's better than the inside of your head. And if this feeling sticks around for days, please talk to a doctor or someone you trust. You don't have to carry it alone.

### Heartbroken

**Take 1** (`heartbreak-1`, ~29s)

Oh, honey. Heartbreak is the worst. It physically hurts, and anyone who says 'just get over it' has clearly never been through it. So you don't have to be over it. Not today. Today you're allowed to be sad, eat the ice cream, and listen to the sad songs. But I want you to remember: the way they treated you is about them. It is not a measure of what you're worth.

**Take 2** (`heartbreak-2`, ~27s)

Hey you. Do not text them. I mean it. Put the phone down. If you need to write the message, write it in your notes app and don't send it. Then text me, I mean, press this button again. Every day you don't text them is a day you're choosing yourself. And one day, sooner than you think, you'll realize you haven't thought about them since lunch.

**Take 3** (`heartbreak-3`, ~26s)

Bestie, I know it doesn't feel like it right now, but you're going to be okay. Better than okay. You're going to look back at this and see it as the moment things changed. Make a list of everything you're going to do now that you don't have to compromise. The trip, the haircut, the hobby they thought was weird. Start one this week.

### I don’t feel good enough

**Take 1** (`not-enough-1`, ~26s)

Hey. I'm going to stop you right there. You are enough. Not when you lose the weight, not when you get the job, not when you finally have it all figured out. Right now, as you are, today, you are enough. You were enough the day you were born, and nothing you've done since has changed that. Say it with me: I am enough. Again. Good.

**Take 2** (`not-enough-2`, ~20s)

Do you know who feels like an imposter? Everyone good. The people who don't worry about being good enough are usually the ones who should. Feeling like a fraud just means you're growing into something bigger than you used to be. That's not a warning sign. That's a growth spurt.

**Take 3** (`not-enough-3`, ~21s)

Okay, here's your homework. Find a message or an email where someone said something nice about you. A thank you, a compliment, anything. Screenshot it. Make a folder called 'Proof.' Every time you feel like you're not good enough, open the folder. The evidence is on your side, bestie. It always has been.

### Feeling lonely

**Take 1** (`lonely-1`, ~21s)

Hey, I'm here. Lonely is such a heavy feeling, and it lies to you. It tells you nobody cares, and that's just not true. So let's prove it wrong. Think of one person who'd be happy to hear from you. Send them a message right now. Even just a heart. Connection starts small.

**Take 2** (`lonely-2`, ~20s)

Hi, bestie. Can I tell you something? Some of my loneliest moments were in a room full of people. Lonely isn't about how many people are around you. It's about feeling seen. So today, let someone see you. Tell one person how you're really doing. Not 'fine.' The real answer.

**Take 3** (`lonely-3`, ~22s)

Okay, lonely night plan. Get out of the house if you can, even just to a coffee shop or a library. Being around people, even strangers, helps more than you'd think. And if you can't go out, call someone instead of texting. Voices matter. That's why I'm doing this in voice notes, you know.

### Can’t sleep

**Take 1** (`cant-sleep-1`, ~26s)

Hey, sleepyhead. Or not-so-sleepy head. Let's get you settled. Put the phone face down after this. Close your eyes. Starting at your toes, I want you to squeeze them tight, and let go. Now your calves, squeeze, and let go. Keep going up, all the way to your shoulders, your face. Everything heavy, everything soft. You don't have to sleep. Just rest. Sleep will find you.

**Take 2** (`cant-sleep-2`, ~26s)

Hi, you. Middle of the night thoughts are the worst, aren't they? Everything feels bigger at two a.m. Here's the thing: nothing you're worrying about can be solved right now. It'll all still be there at nine o'clock, smaller and more manageable. So let's park it. Picture putting it all in a box by the door. You can pick it up in the morning.

**Take 3** (`cant-sleep-3`, ~21s)

Okay, try this. Pick a category, like fruit, and go through the alphabet. Apple, banana, cherry. Your brain can't worry and play the alphabet game at the same time. If you get to Z, start again with dog breeds. I've never made it past M. Goodnight, bestie. I'll see you in the morning.

### Something good happened!

**Take 1** (`celebrate-1`, ~22s)

Wait, what? Something good happened? Tell me everything! Okay, I can't actually hear you, but I'm screaming anyway. I am so proud of you. Now, do me a favor: don't rush past this. Don't go 'oh, it was nothing' and move on. Sit in it. Tell someone. Do a little celebration. You earned this.

**Take 2** (`celebrate-2`, ~21s)

Yes, bestie! This is what I'm talking about! You know what I love? You worked for this. Maybe nobody else saw all the effort, but I know it was there. So tonight, celebrate. Fancy dinner, dance party, a really good bath, whatever feels like a party to you. Wins deserve a moment.

**Take 3** (`celebrate-3`, ~19s)

Okay, I'm so happy for you. Here's a little tradition I want you to start: write this down. The date and what happened. Keep a list of good things. On a rough day, you'll read it and remember that good things happen to you. They just did.

### I can’t get going

**Take 1** (`motivation-1`, ~23s)

Okay, bestie, I see you on that couch. No judgment, I've been there. Here's the deal: we're not doing the whole thing. We're doing two minutes. Stand up, put on your shoes, open the laptop, whatever the first tiny step is. Just two minutes. If you want to stop after that, you can. But I bet you won't.

**Take 2** (`motivation-2`, ~18s)

Hey. Motivation is a liar. It says it'll show up first, and then you'll start. Nope. It's the other way around. You start, and then motivation shows up, like five minutes late with a coffee. So don't wait for it. Start badly. Start messy. Just start.

**Take 3** (`motivation-3`, ~18s)

Okay, I'm counting you down. Five, four, three, two, one, up! Go! You're up? Good. Now drink a glass of water and do the very first thing. Not the whole list, the first thing. I'll be right here when you're done, cheering, like always.

### Out tonight, not drinking

**Take 1** (`out-dry-1`, ~25s)

Okay, you're heading out and you're not drinking. I love that for you. Here's the plan: get to the bar first and order your own drink, something with lime in a nice glass, so nobody hands you one. If anyone asks, 'I'm doing a thing' is a full answer. And you get to remember every single funny moment tonight. Have fun, bestie.

**Take 2** (`out-dry-2`, ~20s)

Hey you! Quick pep talk before you walk in. Remember why you're doing this. Say it in your head right now. Good. Now, give yourself an exit time. If it stops being fun, you're allowed to leave. Nobody remembers who left at ten. But you'll remember waking up feeling amazing tomorrow.

**Take 3** (`out-dry-3`, ~26s)

Psst, are you at the party? Go stand near the snacks. Trust me, that's where the good conversations happen anyway. And if you get that moment where everyone's ordering another round and you feel the pull, step outside for a minute, breathe some cold air, and come back in. You've got this. And if you're tracking your dry days, that's another one for the pile.
